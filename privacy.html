Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

The short version

This site has no ads, analytics or trackers. If you make a Zig account we store what's needed to run it, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing. Your cart is saved only in your own browser.

Zig accounts

Accounts are optional and for people 13 and older. When you join we store your email, your Zig name, your password (scrambled, never readable), your Zags balance and history, and any discount codes you get. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google. This is stored with Supabase, our account provider, which keeps you logged in using your browser's local storage.

When you buy a game

Checkout is run by Lemon Squeezy. They collect your name, email, payment details and billing country to process the order and send your receipt and download link. We receive your name, email and order details so we can help with support and refunds, and to add Zags to your account if you have one with the same email. We never see your full card details. See Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy.

Emails we send

Emails come from ziggfaide@gmail.com, sent through Google's Gmail. Members get account emails (confirming their address, password resets), a welcome email, and a receipt for each purchase. Members also get occasional news about new games and sales, which can be turned off with the link in any news email or in account settings. We don't send email to people without an account, except a receipt when they buy a game.

Faide, the chat helper

Faide is an AI assistant. What you type to Faide is sent to Google's Gemini service to write a reply, and Google may use it to improve its products, so please don't share passwords or payment details in chat. If you're logged in, Faide can also see your own Zags, check-in streak, discount codes and recent activity so it can answer questions about your account, and nobody else's. We don't save your chats; they stay in your browser tab until you close it. If you choose "Send to ZiggFaide", that chat and the email you enter are emailed to us so we can reply.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.

The games

Our games save progress only on your own computer and do not send data anywhere.

Your rights

You can see your details, change them, or delete your account and all its data yourself on the Account settings page. To ask what else we hold about you, or for help, email ziggfaide@gmail.com.
