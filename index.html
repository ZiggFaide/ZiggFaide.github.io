Help

How do I get my game after paying?
Right after checkout you get a download link on screen and by email. The email also has your receipt.
I lost my download link.
Search your inbox for the receipt from Lemon Squeezy. It has the link, and you can download again any time.
Who handles payment?
Lemon Squeezy. They take card, PayPal and Apple Pay, and handle sales tax. Your card details never reach this site.
What are Zigs and Zags?
Zigs are ZiggFaide members. Zags are the points Zigs earn: 500 for joining, 200 for every $1 spent, and more for checking in daily. 1,000 Zags = $1 off. Join free.
Do I need an account to buy?
No. Anyone can buy as a guest. Buy while logged in, or with the same email, to earn Zags.
